Casting controls

In Chrome’s picker, choose Chrome Tab and leave “Also share tab audio” on. A whole app window can be shared without audio. Changing local audio playback may open the picker again; choose the same tab to continue.

The optional ?keepalive=1 mode is active. It plays a very quiet 20 Hz tone locally to discourage background throttling; hardware and browser behavior vary.
